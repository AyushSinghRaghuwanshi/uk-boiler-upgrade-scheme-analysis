THE QUESTIONS

Policy change. Local differences.

Did applications and paid redemptions change after October 2023? Which councils have lower uptake than their housing characteristics suggest?

01

Follow the policy timeline

Segmented regression fits a trend, November level and slope changes, plus annual seasonality. Fifteen pre and six post months remain after excluding launch, September and October.

Three calendar-month HAC lags, small-sample correction and t intervals describe model uncertainty. Air-to-water sensitivity uses the identical specification.

Different grant changes: air-to-water £5,000 → £7,500; ground source £6,000 → £7,500, from 23 October 2023.

02

Compare places fairly

Cumulative hydronic payments are divided by Census 2021 households. Poisson and negative binomial models use household offsets; boosting uses household weights and Poisson loss.

Five geographic outer folds hold out whole regions. Nested selection compares four candidates in four inner folds before evaluating each outer holdout. All predictions displayed are held out.

Coverage: 316 of 318 BUS districts; Barnsley and Sheffield are excluded because a partial boundary transfer prevents a defensible Census match.

03

Know what the data can say

Applications are requests. Paid redemptions record payments after installation, with timing lags. National filters do not apply to cumulative council data.

The window is short, policy was anticipated and no untreated control is credible. A no-break projection is conditional, not a proven causal counterfactual.

Households are an old exposure proxy, not eligible homes. Income, installer supply and affordability are missing. Council gaps depend on the model and are not targets, equity measures or household behaviour.

04

Independent by design

This educational project is inspired by published evaluation work. It is not an official ICF project and is not endorsed by ICF, DESNZ or ONS.

ICF’s reported 74% whole-scheme additionality is not an estimate of the October 2023 grant effect. Our observational analysis cannot reproduce that evaluation.
